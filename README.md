# Speedwatch
Speedwatch shows your browser's live data usage on the toolbar icon and checks your internet speed on a schedule you choose.

The badge updates every second with your current download rate, such as 340K or 1.2M, and the panel adds today's total in KB, MB or GB. Speed checks run every few minutes (you set the interval) and appear as a 2-hour bar chart, with average, peak, lowest and ping. The panel also shows when the last and next checks happen. Speedwatch only measures Chrome's own downloads, not your whole device. It keeps just 2 hours of history, stored locally in your browser.
